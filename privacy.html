TouchPost Privacy Policy

TouchPost is an educational and experimental software project.

TouchPost does not require a user account for its current functionality.

TouchPost does not provide its own photo hosting. Users provide public image URLs hosted by third-party services. TouchPost does not receive or store the actual photo files on its own backend. The public URLs may be used by the TouchPost web viewer to display the selected images.

Third-party image hosting providers have their own privacy policies and terms. Users are responsible for the content and URLs they choose to use.

Third-Party Services

TouchPost may use or interact with third-party services, including:

  • Google AdMob for advertising;
  • third-party image hosting services selected by the user.

TouchPost does not control these third-party services.

TouchPost may display personalized advertising through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process device, advertising, consent, and usage-related information according to their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

TouchPost does not sell uploaded photos and does not provide the developer with direct access to image files hosted by users on external services.
