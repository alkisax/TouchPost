Delete Account – My PDA

My PDA provides in-app account deletion for administrator accounts.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open My PDA and sign in to your administrator account.
  2. Open the Account screen.
  3. Tap "Delete My Account".
  4. Confirm the warning message.
  5. Enter your current password.
  6. Tap "Permanently delete my account".

What Happens After Deletion

Administrator account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

The administrator account and associated organization are permanently deleted from My PDA's database.

The deletion also removes organization data including:

  • Organization memberships
  • Tables
  • Table sessions
  • Reservations
  • Orders
  • Carts
  • Participants and related customer information
  • Related transactions
  • Menu items
  • Categories
  • Addon groups
  • Tags
  • Organization settings

Staff Accounts

Staff accounts that belong only to the deleted organization may also be permanently deleted.

Staff users that also belong to another organization may be retained.

Third-Party Services

Deleting your My PDA account does not automatically delete or disconnect information independently stored by third-party providers such as Stripe or Google AdMob.

After Successful Deletion

The application removes the local authentication token, signs the user out, and returns to the login screen.
